Not currently implemented — intentionally

In Validity DemandTools, Convert performs Salesforce Lead conversion — turning a Lead into an Account, Contact, and optionally an Opportunity.

This module has been deliberately left unbuilt for two reasons:

  • No Doane use case. The student data model is the Person Account, populated through the Colleague → Ethos → Conductor pipeline. There is no Lead-based admissions funnel for a Convert tool to act on.
  • Disproportionate cost. Salesforce exposes no REST endpoint for Lead conversion — it requires the SOAP convertLead() call (or an Apex shim). Building and maintaining that plumbing for a feature with no users would not be a responsible use of effort.

If a genuine need emerges — for example, if Admissions adopts a Lead-based prospect workflow — this tab is the place it would live. Until then, the bulk-data needs of the migration are covered by Import, Export, Modify, Tune, and Match.
